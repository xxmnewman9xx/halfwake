Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Halfwake does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your dreams

Everything you record — text, titles, symbols, people, places, feelings and reflections — is stored only on your device, inside Halfwake's private storage. It is included in your device or iCloud backup if you have backups turned on, under Apple's control. We never receive it.

Voice

When you speak a dream, audio is transcribed on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is never saved or sent anywhere by Halfwake. The first time, iOS may download a speech model from Apple.

Interpretation

Reflections and pattern detection run entirely on your device, using Apple Intelligence where available or Halfwake's built-in symbol library. Your dreams are never sent to a cloud AI service.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We receive no payment information and no personal details.

Widgets and reminders

Widgets show a small summary (such as your latest dream title and streak) stored on your device. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Deleting your data

Delete any dream, or everything at once in Settings › Delete all dreams. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Not medical advice

Halfwake is a journal for reflection. It does not provide medical, psychological or diagnostic advice.

Children

Halfwake is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: contact us.
