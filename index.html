Halfwake

Catch your dreams before they fade. Say what you remember the moment you wake. Halfwake writes it down on your iPhone, notices what keeps coming back, and helps you reflect on what it might mean — privately.

Help & feedback

  • Voice isn't working? Allow the microphone for Halfwake in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. The first time you speak, iOS may download an on-device speech model; this needs a connection once.
  • Interpretations look simpler than expected? Rich AI reflections use Apple Intelligence, available on iPhone 15 Pro and later with Apple Intelligence turned on. Other iPhones use Halfwake's built-in symbol library.
  • Moving to a new iPhone? Your journal travels with your iPhone or iCloud backup. You can also export every dream from Settings › Export all dreams.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Use Settings › Restore purchases in Halfwake after reinstalling.

Anything else? Open a request — we read every one.
